## Copy guardrails

- Say **no added watermark on exported files**, not "completely free" or "unlimited free exports."
- State the account requirement and weekly export allowance near the first install action.
- State that ads and in-app purchases are present.
- Describe on-device handling specifically for document content; do not generalize it into a no-data-collection claim.
- Do not publish an iOS roadmap or release claim until it has a current first-party source.
- Link users to the complete privacy policy for account, analytics, advertising, diagnostics, billing, and notification processing.
