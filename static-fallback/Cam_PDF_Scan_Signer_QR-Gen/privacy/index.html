1. Privacy at a glance

  • Scanning, document editing, signatures, optical character recognition (OCR), QR-code processing, and file generation take place on your device. Our account service does not provide cloud storage for your document content.
  • Files may leave your device when you choose to share, print, export, back up, or open them using another service. That service handles the files under its own privacy practices.
  • In iOS V1, you can select Skip and use the document tools without creating an account. The two-output weekly allowance is stored on your device and is shared by guests and signed-in users. Signing in does not add uses or synchronize that allowance across devices.
  • iOS V1 does not offer advertisements, purchases, reward surveys, quests, usage refills, or push-notification registration. Android includes separate advertising, account, reward, notification, and purchase features.
  • Optional sign-in, analytics, diagnostics, and security services can process information separately from your documents. Using the app without an account does not mean that no technical information is processed.
  • You can initiate account deletion inside the app or contact us through our account-deletion page.

2. Documents and information stored on your device

The app uses local storage to process and retain scans, imported photos and files, page images, thumbnails, signatures, annotations, OCR text, QR-code contents and history, generated files, project indexes, preferences, and temporary working files.

We do not use the account database to upload or store your document pages, signatures, OCR text, QR contents, or exported documents. We do not intentionally include this content in analytics or diagnostic reports. Technical reporting and its safeguards are described in Section 5.

When you use the system share sheet, a file picker, a print service, a photo library, a cloud drive, or another app, your selected destination may receive the content. We do not control copies held by those services or recipients. Your operating system and backup settings may also determine whether local app data is backed up. Android app backup is disabled by the app; iOS device and iCloud backups depend on Apple's services and your settings.

Local documents and exported copies remain until removed through the app, device storage, or the destination service. Uninstalling generally removes app-controlled storage, but does not necessarily remove exported files, operating-system backups, or third-party copies. Account deletion does not automatically erase local documents or shared files.

5. Analytics, diagnostics, and security

Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage and improve the app. When collection is enabled, it may process app-instance or installation identifiers, device and operating-system information, app version, language or region, session information, approximate geographic information derived by the provider, and interaction events such as feature access, sign-in, or export completion. Android may associate a signed-in account identifier with Analytics. iOS V1 build 70 does not attach the authentication account ID to Analytics.

On iOS V1 build 70, usage analytics is off by default. You may opt in or withdraw at any time using the Optional usage analytics switch in Me. This choice is stored on your device and does not change access to document tools or the weekly output allowance. Withdrawing stops new usage events and resets local Firebase Analytics data; it does not automatically erase information already received by a provider. Older iOS test builds enabled Analytics without this switch, so upgrading from one of those builds may retain its native setting until the new app initializes. On Android, the iOS switch is unavailable and the existing Android analytics configuration applies. Selecting Skip, signing out, or changing Android advertising preferences does not itself change analytics consent. Contact us for access, deletion, restriction, or objection as described in Section 10.

Diagnostics

When configured, Sentry receives technical error and reliability information, such as error messages, stack traces, timestamps, app and operating-system versions, device information, session information, and operation context. A signed-in account identifier may be attached to diagnostic events, so these events can be linked to your account.

The iOS V1 app filters recognized local file paths, file URLs, filenames, and common direct identifiers from Sentry events before transmission. These filters reduce accidental disclosure of local file information. We do not intentionally send document contents, signatures, OCR text, QR payloads, passwords, or authentication tokens as analytics events or diagnostic attachments. Diagnostic reporting is active only in releases configured to send reports to Sentry.

Security and infrastructure

Firebase App Check uses platform integrity services, including Apple App Attest for iOS and Google Play Integrity for Android, to help protect connected services. These services process app and installation signals, attestation material, tokens, timestamps, and security information.

Our backend and infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, request metadata, user agents, authentication information, response codes, timestamps, and security logs for authentication, abuse prevention, service operation, and troubleshooting. We use security checks to protect connected services; they do not upload local document contents to our account database.
