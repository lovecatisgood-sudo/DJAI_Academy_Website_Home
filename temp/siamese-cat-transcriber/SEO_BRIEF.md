# DJAI Video to Text SEO brief

## Reader and job

A creator, student, founder, or operations worker has a video/audio file and needs editable text or captions now. Their immediate job is to upload the file, transcribe it, check wording against timestamps, then download the format that fits the next task.

The page serves the tool intent; the primary action stays above the fold. The relevant next steps are subtitle export, DJAI media conversion tools, DJAI courses for people who want to build a workflow, or Siamese Cat Dev for a custom system.

## Chosen query families

English primary: `video to text converter`

English supporting: `transcribe video to text`, `free video transcription`, `video transcription no sign up`, `video to text converter no email`, `MP4 to text`, `video to SRT`

Thai primary: `แปลงวิดีโอเป็นข้อความ`

Thai supporting: `ถอดเสียงวิดีโอเป็นข้อความ`, `ถอดเสียงเป็นข้อความ ฟรี`, `แปลงเสียงและวิดีโอเป็นข้อความ`, `ถอดเทปวิดีโอ`, `ทำซับไตเติ้ลจากวิดีโอ`

These are query families, not a claim of a particular monthly volume, difficulty score, or expected ranking. Keyword-tool and Search Console data should replace these hypotheses after launch.

## Research observations — 2026-08-09

Live search results show several pages targeting `video to text` and `free video transcription`, including established products such as CapCut and Narakeet for Thai-language queries. The results also repeatedly foreground account friction: no sign-up, no account, no email, or no credit card. That makes the no-sign-up promise worth putting in the title, first screen, UI, FAQ, and structured content—but it is a competitive differentiator, not a claim that no competitors offer it.

The page therefore targets one canonical video-transcription workflow rather than manufacturing separate near-duplicate pages for `MP4 to text`, `video transcriber`, and `video to transcript`. Those terms are handled naturally in the title, visible copy, supported-format explanation, and FAQ. Create a distinct subtitle or audio-only page only if it has a distinct working interface and Search Console evidence supports it.

## On-page plan

- Thai canonical: `https://www.djai.academy/tools/video-to-text/`
- English canonical: `https://www.djai.academy/tools/video-to-text/en/`
- Each page includes its own canonical plus reciprocal `th`, `en`, and Thai `x-default` alternatives.
- The HTML contains localized title, meta description, H1, first-screen promise, practical explanation, FAQs, SoftwareApplication, BreadcrumbList, and FAQPage structured data.
- The no-sign-up statement is backed by the implementation: there is no account/email field; an anonymous HttpOnly browser session isolates jobs.
- Do not claim browser-only processing, unlimited use, automatic deletion, or perfect transcription: none is guaranteed by the deployed product.

## Measurement after the service is live

Track Search Console impressions/clicks/CTR/query mix for the canonical URLs, index status, tool-start clicks, completed jobs, export type, deletion rate, clicks to related media tools, course interest, and service-interest clicks. Reassess titles and long-tail coverage only after enough query data exists.
