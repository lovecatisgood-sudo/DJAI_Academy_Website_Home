PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No sign-up does not mean public files.

Your browser receives an anonymous session token. It is not an account, but it prevents other visitors from listing, opening, editing, downloading, or deleting your jobs. Files are stored on the DJAI transcription service only to complete and revisit your selected work; delete a job to remove its source media, processed audio, and exports.
