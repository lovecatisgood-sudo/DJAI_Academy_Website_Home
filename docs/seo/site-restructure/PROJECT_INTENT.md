# DJAI Site Restructure — Project Intent

## Ultimate goal

Improve indexing, intent satisfaction, internal discovery, Cam PDF installs, and qualified development enquiries while preserving every functioning tool, existing public URL, legal route, and the site's familiar visual identity.

## Property ownership

- `www.djai.academy`: Development, Service, Portfolio, Free Tools, Cam PDF, and supporting resources.
- `school.djai.academy`: public vibe-coding/course discovery and authenticated learning.
- No additional tools or Cam PDF subdomain.

## Non-negotiable invariants

- Development is the main domain's primary business journey.
- A free tool's task and result remain above any commercial bridge.
- PDF/document and relevant QR workflows may bridge to Cam PDF after success; unrelated tools do not.
- Existing tool algorithms, inputs, outputs, downloads, privacy behavior, and canonical URLs remain unchanged unless a verified defect requires a separately tested correction.
- Visible UI changes remain minimal: navigation labels, hierarchy, and links may change; the established component styling, headers, and footers remain recognizable.
- New and existing indexable URLs have one primary intent and query-family owner per locale.
- Search volume alone never justifies a page; the page must satisfy a distinct user job with supportable evidence.
- Course URLs remain on `www` until equivalent School pages are live, verified, and mapped one-to-one.
- No many-to-one redirect to a School homepage.
- Canonical, hreflang, sitemap, robots, internal-link, and redirect signals agree.
- Private School content remains authenticated and excluded from public sitemaps.
- No unsupported feature, privacy, performance, price, availability, customer, or result claim.
- Thai and Vietnamese publication requires fluent-human review.
- Analytics never receives filenames, contents, QR payloads, personal data, or free-form input.

## Required outcomes

1. Main navigation exposes Build with DJAI, Free Tools, Cam PDF, Learn at DJAI School, Resources, and one project-enquiry action.
2. Homepage stays visually familiar while explaining the four user journeys and prioritizing Development.
3. Development, Service, and Portfolio have distinct search roles and link together as proposition, chooser, and proof.
4. Tool routes remain task-first and use deterministic related-tool and acquisition links.
5. Cam PDF has accurate product metadata, install links, legal consistency, and evidence-backed workflow content.
6. School provides crawlable Thai and English course discovery before legacy course migration.
7. Public routes are reachable, indexable as intended, and free of broken internal links or redirect chains.
8. Performance and accessibility do not regress materially.

## Explicit exclusions

- No visual redesign or brand rename.
- No tool-domain or Cam PDF subdomain migration.
- No automatic deletion or merging of similar-looking tools.
- No keyword-stuffed pages or one page per spelling/query variant.
- No changes to authenticated learning, billing, account, admin, or classroom behavior beyond public-route protection needed for course discovery.
- No production deployment until its release gate passes.

## Current interpretation

“Headers and footers are right as original” means preserve their established visual components and interaction patterns while correcting labels, destinations, prominence, crawlability, locale behavior, and broken links. It does not mean retaining the existing mixed learning/commercial information architecture.
