Butcher War Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how DEEJAI LAB / DJAI Academy handles information in the Android and iOS versions of Butcher War — War of Hooks. Contact us at contact@djai.academy with privacy questions or requests.

In brief: solo play does not require a game account or joining multiplayer. Progress and preferences are saved on your device. Multiplayer shares your chosen nickname and the information needed to play with others. Google provides advertising and consent services. The current game has in-app purchasing disabled.

Information saved on your device

The game saves your display nickname, selected character, earned Grim unlock progress and ownership, audio preferences and completed-match counters on your device. These records support gameplay, preferences and the game's ad and reward rules. The current game does not provide a player account or cloud synchronization of earned characters.

Clearing app data can remove local progress. Your operating system may back up or restore app data according to your device and account settings. Uninstalling the game does not necessarily remove copies managed by a platform backup service.

Online multiplayer

When you choose multiplayer, the game sends your nickname, room choices, readiness, chosen playable character and gameplay commands to our game server. Other participating players receive the room roster, public nicknames, selected characters, actions, match state, scores and results needed to play together. Room names may appear in the room browser. Please avoid using private information in a nickname or room name.

Our server and hosting providers process connection credentials, IP or network information and technical timing information to operate connections, reconnect players, protect room access and prevent abuse. Optional room passwords travel over encrypted connections and are checked using salted password verification. They are not passwords for a persistent player account. Multiplayer uses HTTPS and secure WebSocket connections.

Advertising, consent and optional rewards

We use Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. These services may process IP-derived approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, diagnostics and privacy choices to deliver or measure ads, manage consent and prevent fraud, depending on your location, choices and platform settings. Requests to obtain consent information also involve communication with Google.

Where required, the game requests current consent information and presents Google's available privacy message before requesting ads. A privacy-options control is available in settings when Google's service requires it. You can use those controls and applicable device advertising settings to review your choices. If consent or an ad is unavailable, the game remains playable.

Optional rewarded ads advance the device's Grim unlock progress only after the ad service reports that a reward was earned. Rewards do not require a purchase. Google's information about its processing and choices is available in its Privacy Policy and advertising information.

Unity and platform services

The game uses the Unity engine. Unity and integrated platform or SDK components may process device, runtime and diagnostic information as described in their applicable privacy notices and the configuration of the released game. We do not promise that the game or its providers collect no technical information. See Unity's game-player privacy information.

Apple and Google operate the app stores, platform advertising controls and relevant backup services under their own policies. The current release has in-app purchasing disabled and does not offer a purchase or restore-purchase flow. This policy covers the game; it does not describe the separate Cam PDF scanner or its camera, document or account features.

Service providers and international processing

We use Railway to host multiplayer, Google for advertising and consent, Unity to run the game, and Apple and Google for distribution and platform services. These providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their processing, retention and available controls are described in their policies, including Railway's Privacy Policy. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to address fraud, abuse or security incidents.

How long information is kept

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until changed, cleared or removed, subject to platform backup and restore behavior. Multiplayer room and match state is held in server memory and removed through leaving, expiry, cleanup or server restart. This is not a permanent match-history or player-account service.

Infrastructure, security and provider records may be retained separately. We retain information we control as reasonably necessary to provide the service, address security or support issues and meet legal obligations. Third-party providers apply their own retention rules. We do not claim that clearing local game data also deletes information independently held by Google, your app store or other providers.

Your choices and privacy rights

  • Play solo without joining online multiplayer.
  • Choose a nickname and room name that do not identify you.
  • Leave multiplayer rooms and use available privacy-options or platform advertising controls.
  • Clear local game data through your device settings, understanding that local progress may be lost.
  • Contact contact@djai.academy to ask about access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent where those rights apply.

Please describe the information or interaction involved so we can understand your request. We may need proportionate verification before responding. Because there is no persistent game account and some records are temporary, we may not be able to identify a past session from a nickname alone. Requests about a provider's independent processing may need to be directed to that provider. Where applicable, you may complain to your local data-protection authority.

Age and younger users

The game is intended for older teens aged 16 and over and adults. It contains fantasy combat, blood and violent effects. Store ratings and minimum ages may be higher in some regions. It is not designed for young children. Do not use the game if you are below the applicable minimum age in your location. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us so we can review the situation and respond as appropriate.

Security and policy updates

We use measures such as encrypted multiplayer connections, access credentials and salted room-password verification to protect the service. No system can guarantee complete security.

We may update this policy when the game, providers or requirements change. The current version will be published at this game-specific privacy URL with its effective date. We will request additional consent where required.
