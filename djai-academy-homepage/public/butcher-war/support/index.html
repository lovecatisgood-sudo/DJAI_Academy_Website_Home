Butcher War Support

Need help with Butcher War — War of Hooks? Email us with your device model, operating-system version, game version and a short description of the issue. Please do not send passwords, room passwords or other private information.

Email: contact@djai.academy

Privacy policy: Read the Butcher War Privacy Policy

Can I play offline?

Solo play can be used without joining online multiplayer. Online rooms and matches require an internet connection and an available game service.

Do I need an account?

The current game does not offer a persistent player account or cloud synchronization. Choose a nickname that does not identify you.

How do I earn Grim?

Grim is earned through optional rewarded-ad steps. In-app purchases are disabled in this release. Ads or rewards may be unavailable because of consent, region, network or ad availability.

How can I ask about my data?

Read the privacy policy or email contact@djai.academy with a privacy request. Local progress may be removed by clearing app data. Multiplayer rooms and match state are temporary; provider infrastructure records may follow separate retention rules described in the policy.
